레벨 5(IM1~IM3) 목표에 딱 맞는 최소한의 고효율 틀입니다.

Part 1

문장 읽기 (Q1 ~ Q2)

  • 감점 방지가 목표! **마침표(.)에서 1초 확실히 쉬어주기**
  • A↗, B↗, and C↘ 나열 억양 패턴 유지하기
Part 2

사진 묘사 (Q3 ~ Q4) - 4문장 완성 틀

1. 장소: This picture was taken at [장소: an outdoor cafe / an office].

2. 인물1: On the left, a man is [행동: drinking coffee / talking on the phone].

3. 인물2: On the right, a woman is [행동: looking at a laptop / carrying a bag].

4. 마무리: Overall, the atmosphere is peaceful [or busy].

Part 3

질문 답하기 (Q5 ~ Q7)

Q5 & Q6 (15초): 질문 문장을 그대로 활용하여 첫 문장 완성 후, because it is convenient / cheap. 덧붙이기

Q7 (30초 만능틀): I prefer [A] for a few reasons. First, it saves my time and money. Second, I can get useful information easily. Therefore, I prefer [A].

Part 4

표 보고 답하기 (Q8 ~ Q10)

Q8 (시간/장소): The event will start at [시간] on [날짜]. It will be held in [장소].

Q9 (정보 정정): Actually, that's not correct. [올바른 정보] is scheduled at [시간].

Q10 (세션 2개 안내): Sure, there are two scheduled sessions. First, [발표자] will talk about [주제] at [시간]. Second, ...

Part 5

의견 제시하기 (Q11) - 60초 만능 조립식 틀

1. 서론: I agree/disagree that [질문 문장 복사].

2. 본론 (만능 이유): First of all, it saves a lot of time and money. Also, it is very convenient to use.

3. 경험 (조립형): When I was a university student, I had a similar experience and it made my life much easier.

4. 결론: Therefore, I think like this.
