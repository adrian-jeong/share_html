Part 4 필승 전치사 & 빈칸 채우기 만능 공식

Q8: 시간/장소/비용/연사
  • • 일시: "It will take place on [날짜] at [시간]."
  • • 장소: "It will be held in/at [장소]."
  • • 비용: "The fee is [가격] per person."
  • • 연사: "The talk will be given by [이름]."
Q9: 정보 수정/취소 정정
  • • 오류 정정: "Actually, you have the wrong information. It starts at [올바른 시간]."
  • • 취소 건: "I'm sorry, but that session has been canceled."
  • • 변경 건: "It was originally [기존], but it has been changed to [변경]."
Q10: 2개 세션 일정 상세 안내
  • • 서론: "Sure, there are two scheduled sessions."
  • • 첫번째: "First, [세션명] is scheduled from [시작] to [끝]."
  • • 두번째: "Second, [세션명] will be held at [시간]."
